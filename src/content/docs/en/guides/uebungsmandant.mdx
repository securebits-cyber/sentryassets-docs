The sandbox is a tenant of its own in your installation, filled with the invented **Beispielfirma GmbH**, a medical care centre: a main house and an outpost with reception, treatment rooms, a laboratory and a server room, people, suppliers, servers, clients, network devices, card terminals and medical devices, licences and contracts, and a Grundschutz++ check it has begun. All names are invented, and the example holds no data about patients. You and your team try everything there without touching your own data. It is part of the Community edition.

## Create and admit

The administration manages the sandbox under **Settings › Sandbox**. There is exactly one per installation.

<Steps>

1. Choose **Create sandbox**. SentryAssets creates the tenant with the standard categories, the example data and the begun Grundschutz check and admits you.

2. Under **Admissions**, choose the accounts that should practise and choose **Admit**. Only active accounts are offered.

3. Admitted people switch in from their user menu with **Into the sandbox**, you also with **Enter sandbox** on this page.

</Steps>

Creating, admitting, revoking and resetting need a fresh confirmation of the sign-in (TOTP or signing in again at the identity provider).

## In the sandbox

A coloured band at the top of every page says: **Sandbox: Changes here affect example data only.** The browser title begins with “Practice”. **Back to your data** in the band or in the user menu takes you back to your own session.

- **Role:** you work with the role of your account, as a base role. Custom roles and rights per location do not apply in the sandbox.
- **Nothing goes outside:** no e-mails and notifications, no rollout, status or SCIM tokens, no agents and collectors, no connections to Active Directory, Entra ID or hypervisors, no help or data assistant. The sandbox's audit log cannot be read there; entering, returning and the end of a sandbox session are in the audit log of your real data.
- **Licence:** the licence of your installation applies. Features from Pro and the add-ons are open in the sandbox only if they are for you. The example data do not count against the number of assets.

A sandbox session ends when your own session ends (sign-out, expiry, new credentials), when your admission is revoked or your role changes, or when the sandbox is reset. Signing out in the sandbox signs you out completely.

## The begun Grundschutz check

Beispielfirma GmbH has begun its Grundschutz check after **Grundschutz++**. You see what a check in progress looks like and practise on the open requirements.

- **Catalog and information domain:** The catalog of the BSI that comes with SentryAssets is read in. The information domain “Informationsverbund Beispielfirma GmbH” covers the main house and the outpost at the normal security level, with MUSS and SOLLTE requirements and target object categories that fit the inventory, such as IT systems, mobile devices, WLANs, server rooms and users.
- **20 requirements answered:** 8 implemented with a responsible person, 5 partly and 5 not implemented, each with “What is still missing?”, a responsible person and a date, and 2 not applicable with a justification. The texts fit the devices of the example company, such as unencrypted workstations in the treatment rooms or one WLAN shared by practice and guests. One requirement is overdue, so the overview shows a task.
- **The rest is open:** several hundred requirements for you to assess.

**Practise the Grundschutz check** in the band at the top leads there. On the **Grundschutz** page a notice leads to **Assess the open requirements**. Go through them step by step; for each requirement the page explains what it means and how to tell it is implemented (see [the Grundschutz check: how to answer](/en/guides/grundschutz/#the-grundschutz-check-how-to-answer)).

- **Edition 2023:** It has not begun, as the compendium of the BSI does not come with SentryAssets. To practise it, read it in inside the sandbox, as in your real tenant.
- **Approvals with four eyes (Pro):** The example answers come from no person but from the system (ID `example`). With Pro they appear under **Approvals** in the stage **To submit**. One person submits, a second admitted person approves; nobody approves alone, as with your real data.
- **Licence:** Grundschutz++ is part of the Community edition, so the check works in every installation.

A sandbox created before this version gets the check once it is reset.

## Reset

**Reset to initial state** deletes everything in the sandbox, its audit log too, and creates it anew with the example data. Admissions stay. The `backup-runner` service does the work; the page shows **Being reset** and then creates the sandbox anew by itself. The reset is in the audit log of your real data. The example data of the sandbox cannot be removed on their own, and the example data of the setup wizard do not go into the sandbox; for a fresh start, reset it.

<Aside type="caution">
A reset cannot be undone. It never touches your own data: the backup service only deletes the tenant that was created as the sandbox.
</Aside>

A backup holds the sandbox like everything else.
