You install SentryAssets once and decide later which edition you need. The functions of the higher editions are visible in the community edition already, with a lock and a short explanation. A licence key or the trial unlocks them at once, without reinstalling and without moving data.

## Which edition fits?

### Community: free and without a limit

The community edition is complete asset management, not a trial version. It is enough if you want to keep your inventory up to date and work out the IT-Grundschutz yourself:

- inventory with locations, owners, relationships, licences, contracts, certificates, racks and documents;
- automatic discovery with agents for Linux and Windows, network scans with the collector and Active Directory sync;
- device condition with a traffic light and tasks;
- IT-Grundschutz in both variants with a guided process, Grundschutz check and evidence;
- backup, audit log and operation with several instances.

The number of assets is not limited. Every function is listed under [Features](/en/reference/funktionen/).

### Pro: less manual work, a better overview

Pro pays off when time is short or several people work together. Above all it takes recurring work off your hands:

| You want to … | Pro offers |
|---|---|
| finish the IT-Grundschutz faster | [protection inheritance](/en/pro/schutzbedarf/) along the dependencies, as a graph and a table, [automatic modelling](/en/pro/modellierung/) of every target object at once, [approvals](/en/pro/freigaben/) in the Grundschutz check with four eyes |
| capture more devices without an agent | [Entra ID and Intune](/en/pro/entra/) and [Proxmox, vCenter and Hyper-V](/en/pro/virtualisierung/) with their virtual machines |
| keep licence costs under control | [licence management](/en/pro/lizenzmanagement/): target and actual per licence, costs over time, budgets and forecast |
| not miss deviations | [check rules](/en/pro/pruefregeln/) across all assets, [workflows](/en/pro/workflows/) and [tickets](/en/pro/tickets/) in Jira, GitLab, YouTrack or Zammad |
| provide evidence for audits | [SIEM export](/en/pro/siem/) of the audit log |
| control rights in detail | [changing roles and permissions and custom roles](/en/pro/rollen-anpassen/), [write permissions per location](/en/pro/standortrechte/) and [SCIM](/en/pro/scim/), so blocked people lose access at once |
| ask questions about the inventory | the [data assistant](/en/pro/daten-assistent/), which looks into the inventory with your permissions |

Pro comes in three sizes, for up to 500, 1,500 or 5,000 assets.

### Enterprise: for large environments with a support contract

Enterprise contains every function of Pro, for environments from 5,000 assets. It adds a support contract with agreed response times, for when you need reliable help in an outage.

### ISMS add-on: the management system on top

The [ISMS add-on](/en/pro/isms/) adds to Pro or Enterprise when you build an information security management system: policy, risk analysis according to BSI standard 200-3, implementation plan, guidelines, security incidents with reporting deadlines, internal audits, management review and the reference documents for an audit.

### Data protection add-on: for data protection officers (in development)

The data protection add-on adds to **every** edition, the community edition included. It leads step by step through the duties of the GDPR, building on your inventory: record of processing activities, processing on behalf, technical and organisational measures, data subject requests, data breaches, data protection impact assessment and deletion concept. The data protection check, the designation of the data protection officer and the record can be used without a licence. The page [Data protection add-on](/en/pro/datenschutz-add-on/) describes its scope. The add-on comes with one of the next versions (see [Roadmap](/en/reference/roadmap/)).

### NIS2 add-on: for entities under NIS2 (in development)

The NIS2 add-on also adds to **every** edition, the community edition included. It leads step by step through the duties under NIS2: scope, registration, the management, risk management measures, reporting of significant incidents, the supply chain, effectiveness and proof. The scope check and the NIS2 check can be used without a licence. The page [NIS2 add-on](/en/pro/nis2/) describes its scope. The add-on comes with one of the next versions (see [Roadmap](/en/reference/roadmap/)).

Prices and purchase are on [sentry-assets.de](https://sentry-assets.de).

## Trial

With the trial you try **every** function of Pro, Enterprise and the ISMS add-on once for 30 days, without a key and without an internet connection.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings › License and edition**.
2. Choose **Try for 30 days**. The page shows until when the trial runs.
3. Enter a licence key before it ends if you want to go on.

</Steps>

Afterwards the community edition applies again. **Your data are kept**; the additional areas are locked until you enter a licence key. With a valid key no trial is offered.

## Entering a licence key

<Steps>

1. You get the key through the shop on [sentry-assets.de](https://sentry-assets.de).
2. Enter it under **Settings › License and edition**.
3. The page shows what the key unlocks, the asset limit and the term.

</Steps>

- The key is checked **offline**, through a signature of the vendor. The installation needs no internet connection for it and sends no usage data.
- One key applies per organisation.
- After the term the community edition applies again; all data are kept.

<Aside>
To run the community edition alone, set `SA_EXTENSIONS=` (empty) in `.env` and rebuild the images. The areas of the extensions are then missing, and so is the trial.
</Aside>
