Every edition is in the same image. Without a licence the additional areas are visible, with a lock and an explanation of what they do; the server refuses their requests on its own. A licence key or the trial unlocks them at once.

## Editions

| Edition | For | Contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Community** | everyone, without a licence key and without a limit on assets | the whole core: inventory, agents, collector, IT-Grundschutz, licences, contracts, racks and more ([Features](/en/reference/funktionen/)) |
| **Pro** | teams with up to 500, 1,500 or 5,000 assets | in addition every function under [Pro and Enterprise](/en/pro/schutzbedarf/) |
| **Enterprise** | large and distributed environments from 5,000 assets | everything in Pro, plus a support contract with agreed response times |
| **ISMS add-on** | adds to Pro or Enterprise | the information security management system ([ISMS add-on](/en/pro/isms/)) |

## Where to find the functions

You find the additional functions in the menu next to the topics they belong to, for example licence management under **Inventory**. Without a licence you see them with a lock and an explanation of what they do.

| Area of the menu | Functions |
|---|---|
| **IT-Grundschutz** | [Protection inheritance](/en/pro/schutzbedarf/), [Automatic modelling](/en/pro/modellierung/), [Approvals](/en/pro/freigaben/), [Check rules](/en/pro/pruefregeln/), [Workflows](/en/pro/workflows/), [Tickets](/en/pro/tickets/) |
| **Inventory** | [Licence management](/en/pro/lizenzmanagement/) |
| **Discovery** | [Virtualisation](/en/pro/virtualisierung/), [Entra ID and Intune](/en/pro/entra/) |
| **System** | [Data assistant](/en/pro/daten-assistent/), after the **AI assistant** |
| **Settings › Sign-in and access** | [Location rights](/en/pro/standortrechte/), [SCIM](/en/pro/scim/) |
| **Settings › Activity** | [SIEM export](/en/pro/siem/) |

## Trial

With the trial you try **every** function of Pro, Enterprise and the ISMS add-on once for 30 days, without a key and without an internet connection.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings › License and edition**.
2. Choose **Try for 30 days**. The page shows until when the trial runs.
3. Enter a licence key before it ends if you want to go on.

</Steps>

Afterwards the community edition applies again. **Your data are kept**; the additional areas are locked until you enter a licence key. With a valid key no trial is offered.

## Entering a licence key

<Steps>

1. You get the key through the shop on [sentry-assets.de](https://sentry-assets.de).
2. Enter it under **Settings › License and edition**.
3. The page shows what the key unlocks, the asset limit and the term.

</Steps>

- The key is checked **offline**, through a signature of the vendor. The installation needs no internet connection for it and sends no usage data.
- One key applies per organisation.
- After the term the community edition applies again; all data are kept.

<Aside>
To run the community edition alone, set `SA_EXTENSIONS=` (empty) in `.env` and rebuild the images. The areas of the extensions are then missing, and so is the trial.
</Aside>
