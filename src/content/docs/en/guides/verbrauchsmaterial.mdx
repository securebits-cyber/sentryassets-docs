Under **Inventory › Consumables** you keep what is not inventoried one by one but counted: toner, cables, adapters and batteries. For each item you see what lies at which location, when to reorder, and which assets the item belongs to, such as a toner and the printers it fits.

Consumables are part of the Community edition.

## Prerequisites

- All roles may view, because stock is not personal data.
- Adding items, booking and assigning assets need a role that may write consumables: **Administrator**, **Information security** or **Editor** (see [Roles and rights](/en/reference/rollen/)).
- At least one location where the stock lies.

## Adding an item and booking stock

<Steps>

1. Open **Inventory › Consumables** and choose **Add item**.

2. Enter the **Name**, such as “Toner HP 26X”, plus **Category** and **Unit** (**Piece**, **Pack**, **Box**, **Cartridge**, **Roll** or **Metre**). **Supplier**, **Article number** and **Note** are optional; the note holds no credentials and nothing about persons.

3. On the item's page, choose **Book**, the kind (**Receipt**, **Withdrawal** or **Stocktaking**), the **Location**, the **Quantity** (for a stocktaking the field **Counted stock**) and the **Date**. **Reason (optional)** and, for a withdrawal, **Received by (optional)** complete the booking.

4. Under **Stock per location**, set the **Minimum**. 0 means: no reminder for this location.

</Steps>

When stock falls below its minimum, the item shows on the overview as a task and in the list under the view **Below minimum**. A stocktaking sets the counted stock and books the difference. Goods delivered for an [order](/en/guides/beschaffung/#orders-without-a-request) are easiest booked in right at the [receipt](/en/guides/beschaffung/#booking-a-receipt-into-stock).

### Bookings and voucher

Bookings are never changed or deleted. Correct a wrong booking with a counter-booking. The **Bookings** table shows the date, location, quantity and the stock after each booking. The **Voucher** column names the request or order whose receipt the booking came from, such as `BE-0003`, with a link to it; for bookings made by hand it stays empty.

Only roles that may read personal field values see the reason and the person who received something; the others see “given”. These roles can also remove a reason, for instance because it names a person.

An item without bookings can be deleted. Archive an item with bookings once its stock is booked out; an archived item takes no booking until you choose **Use again**.

## EAN of an item

The EAN is the number under the bar code on the package. Stored on the item, it lets you find the item again by scanning instead of searching the list.

<Steps>

1. Open the item and choose **Edit**, or create it with **Add item**.

2. Put the cursor in the **EAN** field and scan the package's bar code with a [handheld scanner](/en/guides/scanner/). You can also type the digits; spaces do not count.

3. Save the item.

</Steps>

What is checked when you save:

- **Format and check digit:** EAN-8, EAN-13, UPC-A (12 digits) and GTIN-14 are accepted, digits only and with a valid check digit, so a typo is almost always caught. A 12-digit UPC is stored as the EAN-13 it is, with a leading 0, so that one product has one code.
- **Unique:** within your organisation an EAN belongs to at most one item. The same package at two storage locations is one item with stock at two locations, not a second item.
- **Optional:** items without an EAN, such as cables made up in-house, work as before.

Where the EAN helps:

- The search under **Inventory › Consumables** also finds items by their EAN.
- In the command palette (`Ctrl + K`) a scanned EAN opens the item: the match appears at the top as “EAN …”, and Enter opens it.
- When [receiving by scan](#receive-by-scan) (from Pro) and when [scanning a delivery](/en/guides/beschaffung/#scan-the-delivery) at a receipt, SentryAssets recognises the item by its EAN.

## Receive by scan

<Badge text="Pro" variant="note" /> This section applies from the Pro edition.

Under **Inventory › Receive by scan** you book delivered goods in by scanning them package by package. At the end one click books everything in at once.

<Steps>

1. Open **Inventory › Receive by scan**.

2. Under **Where to**, choose the **Storage location**. The **Date of receipt** is set to today.

3. Scan the packages into the **EAN** field. The field stays ready after every scan; without a handheld scanner, type the digits and press Enter.

4. Check the items and their **Quantity** under **Scanned**. Each further scan of the same item adds one. Change the quantity with **−** and **+** or right in the field; **Remove** takes an item off the list.

5. Choose **Book in (…)**. The number in brackets is the number of items on the list.

</Steps>

### Scanning with the camera

Instead of the hand scanner, the camera of a phone, tablet or laptop can read the barcodes too. Under **Scan**, choose **Switch the camera on**, allow the browser access and hold the item's barcode in front of the camera.

- Each EAN it reads counts as one scan with the hand scanner.
- The same code held in view counts once only. For the next package, take the code out of view for a moment.
- Only EAN codes are read; the camera passes over QR codes and other formats.
- The picture stays on the device and is not sent to the server.
- The camera uses the browser's barcode detection, which Chromium-based browsers offer. If the browser cannot do this, the page says so; use a hand scanner then.

**Switch the camera off** ends the access.

Booking in happens in one step: either every item on the list is booked as a **Receipt** at the chosen storage location, or none is. The message says how many items with how many pieces were booked in. Until then nothing is booked; the list exists on this page only.

### Unknown EAN

If no item has a scanned code yet, the page shows **Unknown EAN …** with three choices:

- **Add item** opens the dialog for a new item with the EAN filled in. If [Icecat](/en/pro/icecat/) is set up, name, manufacturer, category and article number are suggested from the product catalogue; everything stays editable. Once saved, the item is on the list with quantity 1.
- **Assign to an existing item** stores the EAN on an item that exists already, for instance because it was kept without an EAN so far or the manufacturer changed the package. Find it under **Search item** and choose **Assign** on a match. If the item had an EAN already, it is replaced.
- **Discard** skips the code, for instance a bar code scanned by mistake.

### Rights and limits

- The page is shown to **Administrator**, **Information security** and **Editor**. Booking in, adding items and assigning codes need the right to change consumables; the server checks it as for a booking by hand.
- With [location rights](/en/pro/standortrechte/) you also need the right to write at the storage location.
- At most 200 different items per booking and at most 100,000 pieces per item.
- Each booking appears like any other under the item's **Bookings** and in the audit log.

## Reordering and the reorder proposal

When stock falls below its minimum, the overview reminds you to reorder, and the filter **Below minimum** shows the items concerned. **Reorder** on an item opens a new order with that item, see [Reordering](/en/guides/beschaffung/#reordering). From Pro, the [reorder proposal](/en/guides/beschaffung/#reorder-proposal) gathers every item below its minimum by supplier, subtracts what is already on order and creates one order per supplier; with the filter **Below minimum**, **Reorder proposal for … items** leads there.

## Assigning consumables to an asset

An item can be assigned to the assets it is used with, such as a toner to the printers or a drum to the multifunction device. The assignment shows on the asset what belongs to it and how much of it there is.

### From the item

<Steps>

1. Open the item under **Inventory › Consumables**.

2. In the **Used with** card, choose **Assign asset**.

3. Find the asset under **Search asset** and choose **Assign** at a match.

</Steps>

### From the asset

<Steps>

1. Open the asset. The **Consumables** card is in the **Overview** tab.

2. Choose **Assign consumable**, find the item and choose **Assign**.

</Steps>

### What the cards show

- **Used with** on the item lists the assigned assets with a link. An asset you may not read shows as “Asset (no read access)”.
- **Consumables** on the asset lists, per item, the total stock across all locations, marked **Below minimum** where that applies, and the **Reorder** button.
- **Reorder** opens a new order with the item as its first position, with its article number and supplier (see [Reordering](/en/guides/beschaffung/#reordering)). It shows only if you may write procurement and the item is not archived.
- **Remove** detaches an assignment, in both cards. The message “Assignment removed” offers **Undo**.

### Rights and limits

- Assigning and removing are for those who may change consumables, and only for assets of their own organisation that they can see.
- With [rights per location](/en/pro/standortrechte/) (from Pro), the asset's location binding applies: assigning and removing work only for assets at locations where the person may write. The reason: the assignment changes what the asset's page shows.
- An item can be assigned to at most 200 assets.
- Assigning and removing are recorded in the audit log.

<Aside title="Deleting takes the assignment along">
If an asset or an item is deleted, the assignment goes with it. The audit log keeps it: for a deleted asset one entry per assignment, for a deleted item the assigned assets in the entry of the deletion.
</Aside>

## Check the result

- The item is listed under **Inventory › Consumables** with **Stock** and **Locations**.
- The **Used with** card names the assigned assets; the asset shows the item in its **Consumables** card.
- After a receipt, **Bookings** shows a **Receipt** with the number in the **Voucher** column.
- A scanned EAN in the command palette (`Ctrl + K`) shows the item as the first match.

## Troubleshooting

- **“There is not that much in stock at this location.”** A withdrawal cannot take the stock below 0. Check the location, or count again with a **Stocktaking**.
- **“An item with stock cannot be archived. Book the stock out first.”** Book the remaining stock out as a **Withdrawal**, then archive the item.
- **“This item has bookings. Archive it instead.”** An item with bookings is not deleted, so that its history stays.
- **“The item is archived.”** Choose **Use again** before booking.
- **Assigning an asset finds nothing or fails:** you cannot see the asset, or it is at a location where you may not write.
- **“This is no valid EAN; please check the digits under the bar code.”** The length or the check digit is wrong. Scan again or check the digits; if characters other than digits appear, see [handheld scanners](/en/guides/scanner/#keyboard-layout).
- **“Another item has this EAN already.”** Search for the EAN under **Inventory › Consumables** and keep the existing item instead of adding a second one.
- **“… is no valid EAN.”** when receiving by scan: the code is not an EAN, such as a bar code with a serial number on the same package. Scan the bar code holding the EAN.
- **“Booking in failed; nothing was booked.”** Check the storage location and your rights and choose **Book in (…)** again; the list is kept.
- **“The EAN could not be assigned. If another item has this EAN already, change it there.”** The EAN belongs to another item already.
- **“You may not change consumables, so you cannot book in.”** A person with the role **Administrator**, **Information security** or **Editor** books in.
