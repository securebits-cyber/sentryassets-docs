A deviation from a [check rule](/en/pro/pruefregeln/) becomes a ticket in one click on the page of check rules. The page then shows the open ticket with its link.

## Supported systems

Jira (Cloud and Data Center), GitLab, YouTrack and Zammad. Up to three connections per organisation.

## Setting up a connection

<Steps>

1. Open **IT-Grundschutz › Tickets** and add a connection, with a token of its own for SentryAssets.
2. Choose project, issue type or group.
3. Test the connection. The test checks project, issue type or group before any ticket goes there.

</Steps>

The token is stored encrypted and asked for again when the way to the system changes.

## Contents of a ticket

Rule, severity, description and IT-Grundschutz reference, the asset and a link into the inventory. Names from the inventory never reach a ticket as markup, mention or quick action.

## Behaviour

- Tickets are made by the job worker, only in networks the operator allows (`SA_TICKET_ALLOWED_NETWORKS`, private networks by default; `public` for a ticket system in the internet), over TLS 1.3 with the certificate verified.
- Their state is looked up every 15 minutes.
- A failed ticket can be started again.
- The page **Tickets** lists connections and tickets.
