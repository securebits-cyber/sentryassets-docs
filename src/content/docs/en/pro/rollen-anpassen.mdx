The six [roles](/en/reference/rollen/) come with default permissions. With Pro the administration adapts them for its organisation: give a fixed role a permission or take one, change the limit of document confidentiality, or create a **custom role** such as "Purchasing", which may read and file procurement requests.

## Where

Under **Settings › Roles and permissions**. Each column of the table is a role, custom roles on the right with their base ("based on Read only"). What differs from the default is marked: the role with "adjusted", the permission with a dot ("added" or "taken, differs from the default"). The "Permission" column stays in view while scrolling sideways.

Only the administration may change them, with Pro and after a fresh confirmation of the sign-in (one-time code or signing in again at the identity provider, valid for 5 minutes). Information security, auditors and data protection see the table read-only.

## Adjusting a fixed role

- **Set or clear a tick:** applies at once, with **Undo** in the message.
- **Documents visible up to:** choose per role in the row of limits.
- **Restore default** in the column head takes back every difference of that role.

Only the difference from the default is stored. When an update gives a role a new permission, the role gets it even when adjusted.

## Custom roles

<Steps>

1. **Create role**, choose a name (up to 60 characters, unique) and a **base role**: information security, editor, data protection, auditor or read only. The new role at first may exactly what its base may.
2. Set the ticks in its column that it should have more or less.
3. Assign it: local accounts under **Settings › Users**, groups of the identity provider under **Settings › Sign-in** in the mapping of groups to roles.

</Steps>

- At most 20 custom roles. The name may not be that of a fixed role. The base cannot be changed later; create a new role instead.
- **Rename** in the column head, with **Undo**.
- **Delete** works only while no account holds the role and no group refers to it; otherwise the message names the reason.
- When several groups match at sign-in, the base role with the highest precedence wins; with the same base, the fixed role comes before custom roles.
- A mapping in the server configuration (`SA_OIDC_ROLE_MAPPING`) names fixed roles only.

## What the administration keeps

Locked ticks carry a lock and the reason:

| Protection | Permissions | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Administration only | manage users and sign-in, change roles and permissions, organisation and setup (with the licence), backups, system health, granting location rights, managing SCIM | neither taken from the administration nor given to another role |
| Holders only | opinions and the confidential file of the data protection officer in the audit log ([data protection add-on](/en/pro/datenschutz-add-on/)) | stay with the data protection role; the administration cannot give them to itself |
| Stays with the administration | view roles and permissions | may be given to other roles |
| Every role keeps it | sign in and own session, view organisation | without them the interface could not be used |

The administration is no base for custom roles. Other permissions only the administration holds by default, such as managing agents, may be taken from it or given to another role.

## When it applies

- **From the next request** of every person with that role, also in running sessions; the interface then shows only what the person may do.
- When a role loses a permission, SentryAssets drops waiting [e-mail notifications](/en/guides/benachrichtigungen/) of its people that were prepared under the former rights.
- [SCIM](/en/pro/scim/) still only lowers: it compares the permissions as they are now. If an adjustment gives a role more permissions than the former one, the change is no lowering; the person's sessions end and the next sign-in applies the groups.
- Every change is in the audit log (`role.adjusted`, `role.restored`, `role.created`, `role.updated`, `role.deleted`) with the difference before and after.

<Aside>
When the licence ends, adjustments and custom roles **keep applying unchanged**, and custom roles can still be assigned. Only changing them is locked, so nobody gets back permissions that were taken away.
</Aside>
