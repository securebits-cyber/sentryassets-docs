The Community edition matches the computers of Active Directory with the inventory. Pro also reads the **people** of chosen groups, links them to responsible persons and tells you who is new and who is leaving.

Both are under **Discovery › Directory service**: the tab **Computers** in every edition, and from Pro **Users**, **Leaving**, **New joiners**, **Devices** and, for the administration, **Settings**. Without Pro these tabs explain what they bring.

Reading uses the connection you set up and tested under **Settings › Directory services**, with the same service account and read-only. No second service account is needed. SentryAssets changes and disables nothing in AD.

## Setting up

<Steps>

1. Set up, test and activate the [connection to Active Directory](/en/guides/active-directory/) if you have not yet. Important: the connection's base DNs must include the users' OUs too.
2. Open **Discovery › Directory service › Settings** and enter the groups as distinguished names, one per line, such as `CN=Staff,OU=Groups,DC=company,DC=com`. The full name with its `DC=` parts is needed, because they decide which connection reads the group; `CN=Staff` alone is not saved. The group's `distinguishedName` attribute shows it, or `Get-ADGroup Staff` in PowerShell. Members of nested groups count too; at most 20 groups.
3. Add **rules for the department** if needed (see below) and choose the reconciliation interval.
4. **Save and reconcile.** The first reconciliation runs at once.

</Steps>

Only fixed details are read: name, logon name, `userPrincipalName`, email, department, job title, office, company, OU, whether the account is disabled, and when it was created. At most 20,000 people per reconciliation.

With [several domains or forests](/en/guides/active-directory/#several-domains-or-forests) connected, each group is read over the connection of its domain. A group whose domain belongs to no connection fails the reconciliation, so nobody is wrongly taken as gone.

## Department and location from rules

If your AD does not keep the **Department** field, or people should be placed at a location, rules set a department, a location from the location tree or both from the OU or office:

| Rule | Example | Matches |
|---|---|---|
| OU contains | `OU=Sales` | `OU=Team,OU=Sales,DC=company,DC=com`, not `OU=Presales` |
| Office is | `North plant` | office “North plant” (`physicalDeliveryOfficeName`) |

The first matching rule applies, for the department and for the location the first one naming it; a department kept in AD always comes first. Case does not matter. Department and location from a rule are proposals: they reach the inventory only when you create people or take over details (difference **Location**). You also see and change a person's location under **Master data › Responsible persons**.

## Matching with responsible persons

- A person matches a responsible person whose email address equals the email or the `userPrincipalName` in AD.
- **Nothing enters the inventory on its own:** you create new people as responsible persons in bulk and take over differing details (name, email, department, job title) field by field. Empty values in AD overwrite nothing.
- You can link any entry by hand, mark it as “not this one”, ignore it or reset it. Your decision holds over later reconciliations.

## Devices and responsible persons

If **Managed by** (`managedBy`) of a computer in AD names a person of the chosen groups, and the computer is linked to an asset, the **Devices** section proposes that person as the asset's responsible person, provided they are linked in the inventory and the asset has another responsible person or none.

- **Take over proposal** sets the asset's responsible person, with your rights in the inventory.
- **Do not take over** remembers the proposal; you can undo it at once. If AD names someone else later, a new proposal appears.
- Computers whose manager is nobody of the chosen groups, or not linked to a responsible person yet, are counted but not proposed.

## Joiners and leavers

Together with [joiners and leavers](/en/pro/ein-und-austritte/), the page shows two lists, and the bell counts them:

- **Leaving:** the account is disabled in AD or no longer in any group, the person is linked in the inventory, and no leaving is running yet. **Start leaving** opens the case with this person and the day the account was disabled. A leaving completed up to 30 days before the account was disabled counts as well.
- **New joiners:** the account was created in AD within the last 30 days, and no joining is running yet. First **Create as responsible person**, then **Start joining**.

Only administration, ISB and editors see the lists and the bell's tasks. Mail notifications for them are in the category “Handovers and security keys”.

## Rights

| Role | May |
|---|---|
| Administration | groups, rules, reconciliation and everything else |
| ISB, editor | see, link and create people, take over details, start cases |
| Auditor, data protection, viewer | nothing; the people are personal data |

<Aside>
**Remove** deletes groups, rules and every person read, with all decisions. The responsible persons in the inventory stay. A failed reconciliation changes nothing; the reason is shown under **Settings**.
</Aside>
