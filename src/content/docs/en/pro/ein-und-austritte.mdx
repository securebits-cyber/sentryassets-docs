When a person joins, they receive devices, keys and access; when they leave, everything must come back. Under **Employees › Joiners and leavers**, SentryAssets handles both as a case with steps: a **joining** case follows the steps of its template, a **leaving** case also finds by itself what the person still has, and a **transfer** to another department or location finds what they have and lets you decide for each point whether it stays. At the end there is a protocol to sign.

This matches module ORP.2 “Personnel” of the IT-Grundschutz Compendium, which asks for a controlled induction and a controlled procedure when staff leave (source: BSI, IT-Grundschutz Compendium, Edition 2023, module ORP.2). SentryAssets supports the procedure; whether everything is done, you check yourself. Support, not an audit.

## Prerequisites

- A Pro or Enterprise license.
- A role with the right to joiners and leavers: **Administrator**, **Information security** and **Editor** start and work on cases (see [Rights and data protection](#rights-and-data-protection)).
- Cases name who joins or leaves. You see them only with the right to read personal fields.
- The person is listed under **Master data › Responsible persons**; they can also be added when starting.
- Optional: a configured [Entra ID connection](/en/pro/entra/) for the account note when someone leaves.
- Optional: [people from Active Directory](/en/pro/active-directory/). The bell then reports disabled accounts without a leaving and new accounts without a joining, and **Start leaving** proposes the day the account was disabled.

## Adopting a template from the catalog

Instead of starting from scratch, adopt a ready-made template and adapt it. Under **Templates**, choose **From the catalog** and **Adopt** at the template you want. It opens in the dialog and is added only on **Save**, after you have added, removed or reordered steps as you like. If the kind has no default template yet, the adopted one becomes the default. A template of the same name that already exists is marked **Already added** in the catalog.

| Template | For | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Joining after BSI IT-Grundschutz | Joining | Modules ORP.2 "Personnel", ORP.3 "Awareness and training", ORP.4 "Identity and access management" |
| Leaving after BSI IT-Grundschutz | Leaving | Modules ORP.2 and ORP.4 |
| Joining after ISO/IEC 27001:2022 | Joining | Annex A controls such as A.6.1 to A.6.3, A.5.15 to A.5.18 |
| Leaving after ISO/IEC 27001:2022 | Leaving | Annex A controls such as A.5.11, A.5.18 and A.6.5 |
| Joining, simple; Leaving, simple | both | the essentials for the first or last working day |
| Start / End of external staff | both | service providers, temporary staff and interns (ORP.2, ORP.4) |

The templates are suggestions in plain words, not requirements of the BSI or ISO. They exist in German and English, in the language of the interface when you adopt them.

## Adding templates

Templates set which steps a case has, such as “Set up building access” or “Disable account”.

<Steps>

1. Open **Employees › Joiners and leavers** and the **Templates** tab.

2. Choose **Add template**, enter the **Name** and choose **Joining**, **Leaving** or **Transfer** under **For**.

3. Tick **Default template for this kind** if needed. There is one default template per kind.

4. Choose **Add step** and enter a title and, if you like, a description.

5. Choose the kind of step: **Task to check off** or, for joining and transfers, **Issue device**. Tick **Required** if the case must not complete without this step. Optional per step: deadline, approval, mail and ticket (see below).

6. Order the steps with the arrows and choose **Save**.

</Steps>

For leaving, list only further tasks such as blocking access: what the person returns is found by the case itself. At most 50 templates with 100 steps each. Templates apply at every location; a case copies the steps when it starts, so later changes and removing a template leave running cases untouched.

### Deadline, approval, mail and ticket per step

| Field of the step | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Deadline in days** | Days relative to the case's day, from −90 to 90; −3 means three days before, empty means no deadline. The case shows the date and marks overdue steps. If the day changes, all deadlines move with it. The bell and the e-mail notifications report **Steps due soon** (within the next three days) and **Steps overdue**. |
| **Approval by a second person** | Tasks only. Checked off, the step waits for approval; it is done only once another person with the right to cases chooses **Approve**. Whoever checked it off does not approve it. |
| **Mail to** | Up to three addresses, separated by commas. When the case begins, a mail per step goes out with name, kind, day and step, never with the note. It needs the [e-mail notifications](/en/guides/benachrichtigungen/) set up and allowed domains; the case shows whether it went out. |
| **Done by itself** | Tasks without approval only: when the account in Entra ID is disabled, when Intune lists no device with the person as main user any more, or when the account in Active Directory is disabled. The step then counts as done without anyone checking it off. SentryAssets only reads the last reconciliation for this and changes nothing there; checking it off by hand stays possible. |
| **Create ticket in** | A configured [ticket system](/en/pro/tickets/). When the case begins, SentryAssets creates a ticket per step with a fixed title, the step's text and a link to the case, without the note. The case links the ticket or says why there was none. |

### The fitting template by department, location and job title

Under **Applies to** you optionally name **Departments**, **Job titles** (separated by commas, at most 20, case does not matter) and **Locations**, each with everything below it. If you choose no template when beginning, the case takes the one that matches most: department, job title and location of the person under **Master data › Responsible persons**. At a tie the older one applies, without a match the default template. The **Begin case** dialog shows which template fits and why. The automation also takes the fitting template when none is set.

With **Confirmation by the person before completing**, the template requires that the person confirms under **My devices** what they received or returned (see [Confirmation by the person](#confirmation-by-the-person)).

## Joining

<Steps>

1. On the **Cases** tab, choose **Start case** and the kind **Joining**.

2. Choose the **Person from the directory**, or **Add a new person to the directory**.

3. Enter the **First working day** (at most two years away) and choose the **Template**; the **Default template** is preselected.

4. Add a **Note** if needed, without sensitive details such as health.

5. Choose **Start**.

6. In the case, check off each task done. For an **Issue device** step, search the asset, enter **Issued on** and choose **Issue**.

</Steps>

**Issue device** records a handover in the asset handovers, with the same checks as there: right, location, lifecycle and whether the asset is already handed over to someone. Whoever starts the case is responsible for it at first; **Take over** makes you responsible.

## Leaving

<Steps>

1. Choose **Start case** and the kind **Leaving**.

2. Choose the person, enter the **Last working day** and choose the template.

3. Tick **Enter the last working day in the directory** if needed. The overview then reminds of assets still handed over. This needs write access to the inventory and no location binding.

4. Choose **Start**.

5. Work through the points found under **What the person returns** and check off the template's steps.

</Steps>

### What is found by itself

When it starts, on **Look again** and before completing, the case searches the inventory for what the person still has:

| Point | Action | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| **Return**: open handovers | **Take back** with date, **Condition** (**Fine**, **Damaged**, **Lost**) and note | the asset is taken back |
| **Security key** among these handovers | **Open key** and remove the registrations at the key | it is no longer registered with any service |
| **Responsibility**: assets the person is responsible for | **Reassign** to another person or nobody | the person is no longer responsible |
| **Licence seat**: assigned seats | **Free** | the seat is free |

Right after a take-back you can reverse it with **Undo** in the notice, for 15 minutes and only yourself; after that, once handed out again, with a linked protocol or for **Lost**, hand the asset out again if needed. The state of each point comes straight from the inventory every time you open the case. What you do elsewhere, such as taking an asset back on its own page, counts at once. Each action runs with your rights in inventory, handovers and licences and with the asset's location binding; a point you may not read is shown as **Not visible**.

### Account in Entra ID

If a tested [Entra ID connection](/en/pro/entra/) is set up and you may read personal details from Entra ID, the leaving case shows the card **Account in Entra ID**: whether the account linked to the person is still enabled as of the last reconciliation, and how many Intune devices still name them as primary user. SentryAssets only reads Entra ID; it disables or changes nothing there. Disable the account in Entra ID yourself. The note does not hold up completing and reflects the last reconciliation.

## Transfer

When a person moves to another department or location, begin a **Transfer** with the **Day of the transfer**. As for a leaver, the case finds what the person has. For each point you decide: **Person keeps it** (the device stays with them, the point is done) or take it back, reassign or free it as for a leaver. **Decide again** reopens a kept point. You may keep what you could also take back, that is with the write right of the area and at the asset's location. The transfer's template brings what the new place needs; what the transfer issues itself does not count as found. The **Transfer protocol** lists kept points as **Stays with the person**, never as returned.

## Beginning by themselves

Under **Employees › Joiners and leavers**, tab **Automation**, leaving, joining and transfer cases begin by themselves as soon as [Active Directory](/en/pro/active-directory/) or [Entra ID](/en/pro/entra/) show them. SentryAssets checks every 15 minutes.

<Steps>

1. Choose the **sources**: Active Directory, Entra ID or both. The people must already be read there.
2. **Begin leaving cases automatically** for people whose account was disabled or left the groups and who are linked to a responsible person. The last working day is the day of the disabling; it goes into the directory as the last working day where your rights allow it.
3. **Begin joining cases automatically** for people whose account was created within the last 30 days. The first working day is the day it was created. New people without a responsible person are created only if **Create new people as responsible persons on the way** is chosen.
4. **Start transfers automatically** for linked people the directory shows in another department or (AD only, through the location rules) at another location than recorded for them. The day of the transfer is the day it is noticed; the automation takes the new values over into the person. This needs the right to resolve people from AD or Entra ID and the write right in the inventory. A value filled in for the first time is no transfer, nor is the office field, which accounts can change themselves.
5. Choose a **template** per kind or keep **Fitting template, else the default template**, and **Save**. To switch it on you confirm your sign-in first, as the automation then works with your rights for good.

</Steps>

<Aside title="In your name, with your rights">
The automation begins cases in the name of the person who saved last, and only with that person's rights of today. The audit log names this person and notes “automatic from AD” or “automatic from Entra ID”. If the account can no longer sign in or lacks a right, the automation pauses and says so in the bell; it continues only once someone saves the settings again.
</Aside>

- **Never twice:** if a case of this kind is running, no second one begins. If you cancel a case that began by itself, it does not begin again by itself for the same day, even if you changed its date before.
- **One transfer per destination:** the same move of a person to the same new department or location begins once, whether the case was completed, cancelled or the takeover failed. What differs already when switching on is the starting point. If a person's accounts, or AD and Entra ID, show different departments, nothing begins. Someone joining or leaving gets no transfer on top.
- **Safeguard:** each run begins 20 cases at most. If more than 20 leavers were due at once, or more than three and at the same time more than a tenth of the people, nothing begins and the automation pauses with the note “unusually many leavers”. Check groups and base DNs then. The same holds for transfers: begin a reorganisation by hand.
- **Nothing in AD or Entra ID:** SentryAssets still never creates, changes or disables accounts there.
- Roles that may begin joining and leaving and read personal data, without a location binding, and that may read the chosen source, may change the automation.

Cases that begin this way carry the note **automatic from AD** or **automatic from Entra ID** in the list.

## Confirmation by the person

Under [My devices](/en/pro/selbstbedienung/), a person with a linked account sees in the section **To confirm** what a running case issued to them or took back through the case, and chooses **Confirm receipt** or **Confirm return**. The case shows **Confirmed by the person on …** at the step. What the person keeps, or what was returned directly in the inventory, needs no confirmation.

- The person confirms only their own steps and can change nothing else. The confirmation does not replace a signature on the protocol.
- Whoever issued or took back the step, and whoever linked the account to the person by hand, does not confirm in their place.
- If a take-back is undone, its confirmation goes with it.

## Completing

Choose **Complete** once everything is done:

- for **joining**, as soon as all required steps are done;
- when no step waits for approval and, if the template asks for it, the person has confirmed everything;
- for a **transfer**, when every point found is kept or done;
- for **leaving**, in addition when no handover is open any more and every point found is done. Only someone who may read handovers, inventory, licences and security keys completes a leaving case; otherwise something could be overlooked.

Afterwards the case can no longer be changed. **Cancel case** removes a running case with its checked steps after confirmation; handovers and changes in the inventory stay.

## Protocol

**Print protocol** opens the **Joining protocol** or the **Leaving and return protocol** with the organisation's logo: person, department, job title, date, the points with **Inventory and serial number**, date and state, a confirmation and fields for the signatures of the employee and of IT. Print it and have it signed. An issued device is listed by its name with the template step below it; optional steps not done, such as a device not issued, are left out of the protocol. File the signed copy as a document if needed; it then has its own retention.

On the person's page under **Master data › Responsible persons**, the card **Joiners and leavers** shows the running or last case.

## Rights and data protection

| Role | Templates | Cases |
|---|---|---|
| Administrator, Information security, Editor | view, add, change | view, start, work on, complete, cancel |
| Data protection | view | view |
| Auditor | view | – |
| Read only | – | – |

- **Cases** also need the right to read personal fields, because who joins or leaves, and when, is personal. The auditor therefore sees only the templates.
- **Names of users** (responsible, done by) show only to roles that see the names of persons in the audit log: Administrator, Information security, Data protection and Auditor.
- Only someone not bound to locations changes templates. Cases are not bound to locations; every action on an asset still checks its location ([rights per location](/en/pro/standortrechte/)).
- **Audit log:** start with person, template and how it was chosen (chosen, fitting, default), without kind and date; approvals, keeping, confirmations, mails (number of recipients only) and tickets (without text); date changes only as “changed”, notes only as “noted”, never titles or names. Actions in the inventory write its entries as well.
- **Deleting:** if a person is deleted from the responsible persons, their cases go with them. There is no purpose for keeping the name afterwards (Art. 5 and Art. 17 GDPR). The asset handovers still record that an asset was handed out and came back, only no longer to whom.
- Do not put sensitive details into notes, such as health or reasons for leaving.

## Limits

- At most one case of each kind runs per person.
- At most 200 points are found per area and at most 500 steps stored per case. The list shows the newest 500 cases.
- Archiving a person after leaving is not available; they stay among the responsible persons with their last working day until deleted.
- SentryAssets does not block access in other systems. Add such tasks to the template, or use [SCIM](/en/pro/scim/). {/* open: handing access over to SCIM or tickets */}

## Check the result

- The case is listed on the **Cases** tab with **Kind**, **Date**, **Steps** (“… of …”) and the **State** **Completed**.
- After a leaving case, the person has no open handovers left in the asset handovers.
- The protocol names every device with inventory and serial number.

## Troubleshooting

- **“A case of this kind is already running for this person.”** Open the running case, for example from the card on the person's page.
- **“Assets are still handed over.”** Take the assets back under **What the person returns** with **Take back**.
- **“Required steps are still open.”** or **“Points found are still open.”** Finish the open steps and choose **Look again**.
- **“Some points are not visible to you.”** You lack read access to handovers, inventory, licences or security keys. Someone with these rights completes the case.
- **“The asset is already handed over to someone.”** Choose another asset, or take the existing handover back first.
- **“The asset is out of use.”** Disposed assets cannot be issued.
- **“You lack the rights or the location for this.”** The action needs write access at the asset's location.
- **“Steps are still waiting for approval.”** Another person with the right to cases chooses **Approve** at the step.
- **“The person has not yet confirmed everything …”** Ask the person to confirm under **My devices**; if their account is not linked, link it under [Self-service](/en/pro/selbstbedienung/).
- **Mail not sent:** the case gives the reason, such as notifications not set up or a domain not allowed. The mail is not sent again.
- **No ticket:** the case gives the reason, such as missing rights at the ticket system or a connection switched off.
- **“The date is too far away.”** The day may be at most two years from today.
- **No cases visible:** you lack the right to read personal fields.
