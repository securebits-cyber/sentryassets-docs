<Badge text="Pro" variant="note" /> This feature is available from the Pro edition, for Edition 2023 of the IT-Grundschutz.

In the community edition you take module suggestions over one at a time. Automatic modelling saves you the repetition.

## Features

- **Every suggestion at once:** every open module suggestion is taken over in one step.
- **Own rules:** give a category, own ones included, fixed modules, such as the module SYS.4.1 "Printers, copiers and multifunction devices" for your printer category.
- **Protection of target objects:** derived from what a target object stands for: an asset, the assets of a category, a location. The information domain gets the highest value. Taken over on request, with "Undo".

The page **Automatic modelling** shows beforehand what will change. Check the modelling afterwards in the [Grundschutz check](/en/guides/grundschutz/); it remains your professional decision.
