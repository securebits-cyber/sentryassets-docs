<Badge text="Pro" variant="note" /> This feature is available from the Pro edition, for Edition 2023 of the IT-Grundschutz.

In the community edition you take module suggestions over one at a time. Automatic modelling saves you the repetition.

## Features

- **The chosen suggestions at once:** all chosen module suggestions are taken over in one step. The button says what happens: **Take over … modules for every domain** while exactly the modules for every domain are chosen, otherwise **Take over … chosen**. The header with the count and the button travels along as you scroll.
- **For the information domain by how widely they apply:** the suggestions stand in the groups **Applies to every information domain**, **Only if it applies to you** and **Further suggestions**, each module with the sentence on when it applies. Only the first group is preselected, since SentryAssets cannot know whether, say, classified information (VS-NfD) or process control occurs at your organisation.
- **Other target objects stay preselected:** their suggestions follow from the inventory or your own rules.
- **Changing the choice:** **Deselect all**, or **None** at a target object, takes many out at once. **Restore the preselection** appears as soon as the choice differs from the preselection and, like **Preselection** at the information domain, goes back to it; there is deliberately no button that chooses everything there. **Deselect all** also applies to suggestions that arrive later, until you leave the page, and what you deselected stays deselected after a takeover.
- **Own rules:** give a category, own ones included, fixed modules, such as the module SYS.4.1 "Printers, copiers and multifunction devices" for your printer category.
- **Protection of target objects:** derived from what a target object stands for: an asset, the assets of a category, a location. The information domain gets the highest value. Taken over on request, with "Undo".

The page **Automatic modelling** shows beforehand what will change. Check the modelling afterwards in the [Grundschutz check](/en/guides/grundschutz/); it remains your professional decision.
