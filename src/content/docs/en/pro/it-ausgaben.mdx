**IT estate › Inventory › IT spending** shows what IT spent: on procurement, licences and contracts, per period and in total, every figure net and gross.

## Prerequisites

- Pro is active, see [Licence and editions](/en/guides/lizenz/).
- You may read reports; every role may. Procurement, licences and contracts appear only if you may read them otherwise too. If a right is missing, the page says what is left out.
- Licences and contracts carry cost, interval, start and whether the amount is net or gross, see [Licences](/en/guides/lizenzen/).

## View the spending

<Steps>

1. Open **IT estate › Inventory › IT spending**.

2. Choose the period: **Month**, **Quarter**, **Half-year**, **Year** or **Total**. For month, quarter and half-year, choose the **Year** next to it.

3. Read the sum of the period and per source at the top, below it a chart (net, coloured by source; net and gross when pointing at a bar) and the table with every row net and gross.

</Steps>

Currencies are never added together; with more than one, choose it above the chart.

## How it is computed

- **Procurement** counts on the day it was ordered, without an order on the day it was received. Requested, rejected and withdrawn ones do not count.
- **Licences and contracts** count on each day their cost fell due since they began: at the start, then per interval, up to today or their end. One-time costs count at their start.
- **Net and gross** use each item's own VAT rate, see [VAT](/en/guides/einrichtung/#vat).

## Not included

Items that cannot be placed are listed under **Not included** with a link: without net or gross, without a start or without an interval. Add the missing detail and they count.

## Limits

- The overview reaches back 50 years at most; one item counts at most 1,200 times.
- More than 5,000 licences, 5,000 contracts or 20,000 priced purchases are refused rather than summed incompletely.
- Each person may open it 30 times a minute.
