<Badge text="Pro" variant="note" /> This feature is available from the Pro edition.

The administration binds people to one or more locations. A bound person then adds and changes assets, racks, stock bookings and locations **only there and below**.

## What applies

- **Writing** only at the bound locations and everything below them.
- **Reading** stays as the role allows, across the whole tenant.
- A bound person changes nothing that applies to the whole organisation: categories, fields, numbering, lifecycle rules, consumable items, the GLPI import.
- On an asset at another location the interface shows no buttons to change, delete, hand out or dispose of it, but the note "Can be changed only at your locations." A new asset needs one of the person's own locations; without a location, SentryAssets refuses to create it with a note on the missing permission.
- The binding applies from the next request on, never to the administration. The administration cannot be bound; if a bound person later joins the administration, the list shows their binding as “Without effect”.
- It also keeps applying after the licence has ended; only changing bindings needs the licence.

<Aside>
If all of a person's locations were deleted, they write nowhere instead of everywhere again.
</Aside>

Bindings are managed on the page **Location rights**.
