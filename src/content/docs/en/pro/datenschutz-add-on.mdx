The data protection add-on leads organisations and their data protection officers step by step through the duties of the GDPR and the German BDSG. It builds on your inventory: you choose systems, suppliers and people instead of entering them again. With the add-on, the group **Data protection** appears in the navigation, divided into **Build**, **Run** and **Review and report**.

<Aside type="caution" title="Support, not legal advice">
The add-on structures and documents. Whether processing is lawful, a notification is needed or a risk acceptable is for your organisation and your data protection officer to decide.
</Aside>

## Without a licence

The **data protection check** with about 40 questions, whether a **data protection officer is required** together with their designation, and the **record of processing activities** (Art. 30 (1) GDPR) with CSV export can be used without a licence. Everything else shows a lock without one.

## Build

| Area | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | whether an officer is required, designation of the data protection officer and the deputy with the signed designation as evidence and notification to the authority, conflict of interest, responsibilities |
| **Policy and commitment** | data protection policy from a template, approved by the management; commitment of employees to confidentiality per person |
| **Templates to sign** | management resolution on the policy and designation of the data protection officer, filled in from your entries, as print view or PDF |
| **Record** | activities with legal bases, data subjects, data, recipients, third countries, retention, systems and suppliers (the locations follow from the systems and appear in the print view and CSV export too); 21 templates of typical activities, balancing of interests, gaps per activity, print view |
| **Privacy notices** | register of the information duties under Art. 13 and 14 GDPR per activity |
| **Processors** | selection, checks with follow-up, instructions, sub-processors and third countries; shared with the ISMS add-on |
| **TOM** | technical and organisational measures per the Standard Data Protection Model, with the state of the IT-Grundschutz modules (only modules that are in the Kompendium) and evidence from SentryAssets: security state reported by the agents, multi-factor methods, mandatory TOTP for local accounts and the check of the audit log’s hash chain |
| **Risk model** and **impact assessment** | threshold analysis per the mandatory list of the German supervisory authorities, data protection impact assessment per Art. 35 (7) GDPR, opinion of the data protection officer, management decision, prior consultation |
| **Deletion concept** | deletion classes per DIN 66398 with templates, rules per activity and data category, implementation per system, backups and archives, review |
| **As a processor** | record per Art. 30 (2) GDPR, where you process on behalf of others yourself |

## Run

| Area | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Data subject requests** | register with deadlines (one month from receipt; extended, two further months from the end of the first, only where the extension was given notice of within the first month), procedure and letter templates; optionally an inbox that only reads a mailbox through Microsoft Graph or IMAP |
| **Data breaches** | together with the ISMS security incidents: assessment, suggestion on the duty to notify, notification to the authority within 72 hours (the document a recorded notification names as evidence then cannot be deleted and keeps its file, title and kind), record per Art. 33 (5) GDPR. A report recorded by mistake can be removed within the first 30 days. When the ISMS removes a report under the GDPR, the incident shows a notice, and it stays under **Tasks** until data protection chooses **Acknowledge** |
| **New projects** | departments report new software, suppliers or purposes; the data protection officer reviews them with a checklist and gives an opinion; approving despite open items needs its own confirmation |
| **Trainings** | trainings with attendees, import of lists, due date per person and a **training record to sign** |
| **Record of advice** | advice, opinions and information with the recommendation and the management decision: followed, partly followed or deviated from, with a reason |
| **Confidential file** | concerns of employees and data subjects, see below |
| **Supervisory authority** | inquiries, complaints, consultations, notifications and inspections with deadline, handling and correspondence from the documents |

| Area | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Reviews** | reviews by the data protection officer with checklist, findings and corrective actions; the internal audits of the ISMS can be shown for reading |

## Tasks and e-mail

Deadlines and follow-ups appear under **Tasks**, in the bell and, when subscribed in the profile, by e-mail in the category **Deadlines and follow-ups in data protection** (see [E-mail notifications](/en/guides/benachrichtigungen/)). Each task names only a count and leads to the matching page of the add-on.

| Task | When |
|---|---|
| Report to the authority open (red) | a breach is recorded and its report under Art. 33 GDPR is not recorded yet |
| Data subjects still to be informed (red) | where the risk to the persons is high, until the notice under Art. 34 GDPR is recorded |
| Data subject request overdue (red) or due within 7 days | open requests by their deadline |
| Matter with the authority due | deadline passed (red) or within 7 days |
| Corrective action from data protection reviews overdue | the action's date has passed |
| Review due | TOMs, deletion concept, processing activities, impact assessments and guidelines; check of processors |

Data protection and the administration see these tasks while the add-on is licensed; data subject requests and matters with the authority only with the right to data subject requests. A review that never took place is no task but an open point of the guided way.

## Who may do what

| Role | Rights in the add-on |
|---|---|
| Data protection (`dpo`) | everything; only they word opinions, the record of advice, reviews and the activity report, and change the confidential file |
| Administrator | reads everything and changes the other areas; sees the confidential file, every look is recorded |
| Information security | reads, without data subject requests, matters with the authority and the confidential file; reports new projects |
| Audit | reads like information security |
| Editor | reports new projects and sees only their own |

The exact rights are listed under **Settings › Roles and permissions** ([Roles and permissions](/en/reference/rollen/)).

## Confidential file

Employees and data subjects may turn to the data protection officer directly (Art. 38 (4) GDPR); their identity is confidential (§ 6 (5) BDSG).

- Name, contact, description, notes and attachments are encrypted one by one. A database dump or a backup alone does not show them.
- The list shows no names. Search, command palette, exports, webhooks and the help assistant do not reach the file.
- When the administration opens a concern or downloads an attachment, the data protection officer sees it under **Looks**, with person and time.
- A mail from the inbox can be taken over as a concern. The inbox then empties it and does not read the same mail again; delete it in the mailbox itself.

<Aside type="note" title="Sealed entries in the audit log">
The entries of the file stand in a sealed area of the audit log that only the data protection officer sees. The file export and the [SIEM export](/en/pro/siem/) contain only placeholders in their place, with time, area and hash, so that the chain can still be verified. Whoever has the server and the master key can still read the file.
</Aside>

## Activity report

The data protection officer reports to the highest management level (Art. 38 (3) GDPR), usually once a year.

1. Under **Data protection › Activity report**, everyone with read access sees the **key figures** for a period of their choice, three years at most: advice and management decisions, concerns, requests, data breaches, reviews, trainings, matters with the authority, projects, record and deletion concept. Only numbers, no names or contents; what your role may not read stays empty.
2. The data protection officer creates a report, writes the assessment and recommendations and counts the key figures again where needed.
3. With **Submit**, the key figures are fixed. Until the management has taken note, the report can be withdrawn.
4. The **print view** has a field for the management's signature. Upload the signed copy as a document. **Save as Markdown** gives you the report as a text file to edit further.
5. Under **Acknowledgement by the management**, the data protection officer or the administration records date, person and the signed copy. Date and person then stand; the signed copy can follow later. An acknowledged report is kept as evidence and can no longer be deleted. The document of the signed copy cannot be deleted, not even after its retention period, and keeps its file, title and kind; confidentiality and retention can still change.

Figures marked “now” give the state on the day of counting, not at the end of the period.

## Getting started

Start on the **Data protection** home page with the guided way: each step shows what is missing and leads to the page that closes the gap. In the **data protection check**, each gap (“partly”, “no”, “don't know”) carries a link to the page where you close it, such as the policy under **Policy and commitment** or training under **Trainings**. You can try the add-on during the [trial](/en/guides/lizenz/#trial).
