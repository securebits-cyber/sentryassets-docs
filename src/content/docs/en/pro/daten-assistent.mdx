The data assistant is an **AI**. It answers questions about your own data in your own words, such as "Which servers with a high protection requirement have no responsible person?". It uses the language model set up for the [help assistant](/en/reference/ki-assistent/).

<Aside type="caution" title="Data go to the model's provider">
Unlike the help assistant, the model receives **results from your inventory** here: up to 25 assets with fixed fields such as name, category, location and health, counts, software figures and open deviations. It never receives notes, documents, credentials or settings. Use a model you trust with these data, such as your own model in your own network.
</Aside>

## How an answer is made

1. The model chooses up to three **read-only** queries from a fixed list: find assets, count assets, find software, open deviations of check rules.
2. SentryAssets checks each query strictly and runs it with the **asker's rights**.
3. The model answers only from the results. The results are listed below the answer with their filters, numbers and a link to the asset list.

Results can start no further query. Answers with links or hidden characters are not shown.

## Switching it on

<Steps>

1. Set up the [help assistant](/en/reference/ki-assistent/) first.
2. The administration switches the data assistant on separately on the page **Data assistant**, after confirming the sign-in.
3. Optional: names of responsible persons go along only with a **second switch**.

</Steps>

- The consent holds for the model's endpoint at that moment. If the endpoint changes, it receives nothing until agreed again.
- Neither question nor answer is stored.
- Administration, information security, editors and auditors may ask; at most 20 questions per person and hour.
- The assistant has no writing queries and never changes data.

AI answers can be wrong; the listed results are the basis. Support, not an audit.
