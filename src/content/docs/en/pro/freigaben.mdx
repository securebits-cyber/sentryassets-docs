A recorded state in the Grundschutz check can be submitted for approval with **Submit**. Information security or administration approve it or return it with a reason.

## Rules

- **Four eyes:** whoever submitted, or changed state, justification or evidence since the last approval, cannot approve it.
- **What is approved is what was seen:** if state, justification or evidence change after submitting or approving, including a new version of an evidence document, the state is due again. The responsible person, the date and the note do not count.
- A change made while a decision is pending waits for it and then counts against the person who made it.
- Submissions waiting for a decision are always listed first.

Every submission, approval and return is in the history and the audit log; names only for roles allowed to read them. The page **Approvals** lists everything waiting for a decision.
