With SCIM 2.0 your identity provider, such as Entra ID, Okta, Keycloak or Authentik, provisions users and groups to SentryAssets.

## What SCIM does

- A person blocked or deleted there loses access here **at once**, sessions included.
- Leaving a group lowers the role at once if the remaining groups allow less; without a mapped group, access ends.
- If the remaining groups give a role that allows neither less nor more, such as editor instead of data protection, the role stays, but all the person's sessions end; the next sign-in brings the new role. The audit log records it.
- **SCIM never grants:** access and higher roles still come only with a signed OIDC sign-in. A stolen token can lock people out but give nobody rights.
- The last administrator is never blocked.

## Setting up

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings › SCIM** and create a token. It is shown once, stored as a hash, expires after a year at most and can be revoked.
2. Enter the address shown on the page and the token at the identity provider.
3. Start provisioning at the identity provider.

</Steps>

Provisioned users match accounts by the OIDC claim `sub`, for Entra ID by `oid`. The page shows who is provisioned and who has signed in already.

<Aside type="caution" title="Fresh confirmation">
Creating and revoking tokens and changing the matching claim work only shortly after confirming the sign-in: a one-time code for local accounts, signing in again at the identity provider for OIDC, valid for 5 minutes. If it is older, the page shows the confirmation at the top and locks the buttons until then. A token ends access and lowers roles; an unattended session should neither create one nor switch it off.
</Aside>

<Aside>
The mapping of groups to roles stays the one of the [sign-in](/en/guides/einrichtung/#sign-in-with-oidc-optional).
</Aside>
