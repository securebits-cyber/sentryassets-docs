The requirements of IT-Grundschutz say **what** must be achieved, not **how**. The implementation aid of the ISMS add-on closes this gap with technical measures: a measure such as “encrypt disks” is described once and mapped to the requirements of both editions, the Edition 2023 and Grundschutz++.

## What the aid shows

- **Below each matching requirement** on “check implementation”: the goal, steps per platform (Windows and Active Directory, Microsoft 365 with Entra ID and Intune, Linux, macOS, network), **How to tell the measure works**, **What evidences the measure**, effort and prerequisites. Above the measures it says that they only support the requirement.
- **Where it applies to you:** the target objects of your modelling and the assets behind them, in a sentence such as “This concerns 12 assets you are using at the moment.” From Grundschutz++ come the assets in the categories you chose for the information domain. If the inventory lists nothing as in operation, the page says so too.
- **Detection:** for encryption, Secure Boot, screen lock, operating system updates, malware protection, firewall and multi-factor sign-in the aid says how far the devices are according to their agents, with the compliance from Intune and the number of accounts with MFA from Entra ID (Enterprise). From that follows a **suggestion** for the state of implementation, never set by itself. The detection names its limits: signatures up to 7 days old and updates up to 35 days back count as fulfilled, so that devices that were switched off do not keep standing out. The requirement may ask for more.
- **Into the implementation plan:** take a measure up with a person responsible, a date and the assets.
- **Pages of its own** under **ISMS › Implementation aid**, also linked from the **ISMS** start page: per measure the background, common pitfalls, the requirements it supports and sources, arranged by module or practice.

<Aside type="caution" title="Support, not fulfilment">
A measure supports a requirement but does not fulfil it by itself: it serves many requirements and often covers only part of each. The BSI's text is what counts. The measures are aligned with it but remain recommendations of SentryAssets, not prescriptions of the BSI and no legal advice. Whether a requirement is met is for you to judge.
</Aside>
