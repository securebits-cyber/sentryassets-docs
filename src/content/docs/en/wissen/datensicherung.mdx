Only a working backup helps against ransomware, defects and mistakes. IT-Grundschutz asks for a backup concept with regular restore tests (module CON.3).

## What matters

- **3-2-1:** three copies, two media, one off site.
- **One copy offline or immutable**, so an attack with administrative rights cannot delete it.
- **Encrypted**, with a key that is reachable after an outage too.
- **Practise the restore** and measure how long it takes.
- **Include cloud services** such as Microsoft 365.
