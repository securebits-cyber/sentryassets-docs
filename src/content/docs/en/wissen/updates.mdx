Most successful attacks use flaws that were fixed long ago. IT-Grundschutz asks for regulated patch and change management (module OPS.1.1.3) and for replacing systems that no longer get updates.

## What matters

- **Automatic and with short deadlines**, for operating systems and applications.
- **Do not forget servers:** fixed maintenance windows instead of “not now”.
- **Find devices falling behind:** paused updates, disabled services, devices that are rarely online.
- **Keep the end of support in view** and plan the replacement in time.
- The SentryAssets agent reports whether updates come by themselves and when updates were last installed.
