Protection against malware stops much before it does harm. It only works while it runs, has current signatures and someone reads its alerts (module OPS.1.1.4).

## What matters

- **Active on every device** and protected against being switched off by users.
- **Current signatures**, ideally several times a day.
- **Few exclusions**, reviewed regularly.
- **Evaluate alerts** instead of leaving them in a console.
- **Reduce the attack surface:** restrict macros and scripts from the internet.
