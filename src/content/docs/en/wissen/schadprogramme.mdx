Protection against malware stops much before it does harm. It only works while it runs, has current signatures and someone reads its alerts (module OPS.1.1.4).

## What matters

- **Decide per system:** whether a system needs anti-virus depends on its operating system and purpose. “On every device” would be stricter than what the BSI asks.
- **Where it is intended, a product for businesses:** with service, support, central management and real-time scanning, protected against being switched off by users. Check whether your licence of the built-in protection provides this; a scanner without a support contract is not enough.
- **Current signatures**, as the vendor recommends, as a rule no more than a day old.
- **Few exclusions**, reviewed regularly.
- **Evaluate alerts** instead of leaving them in a console.
- **Reduce the attack surface:** restrict macros and scripts from the internet.
