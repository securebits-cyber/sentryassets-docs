A lost laptop without encryption gives away all its data: the disk can be removed and read elsewhere. IT-Grundschutz therefore asks for encryption of laptops and removable media (modules SYS.3.1, SYS.2.1) and a concept of which methods are used (CON.1).

## What matters

- **All fixed drives**, not only the system drive, and servers too, whose disks are replaced and disposed of.
- **Keep recovery keys in a central place**, or the data is lost to you as well after a defect.
- **Current methods** after the BSI's technical guideline TR-02102.
- **Control:** which devices are encrypted and which are not; the SentryAssets agent reports this for Windows (BitLocker) and Linux (dm-crypt).
