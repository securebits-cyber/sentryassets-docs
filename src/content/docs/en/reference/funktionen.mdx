This page describes the community edition. Features from Pro on are under [Pro and Enterprise](/en/pro/schutzbedarf/).

## Overview and tasks

The **Overview** is the start page: the next sensible step, progress in the IT-Grundschutz, key figures compared with the previous week, the sources of discovery and the **Tasks**. Tasks arise from deadlines and rules, such as licences and contracts running out, support ending, backup proofs missing, medical device checks due, requirements due or consumables below their minimum; with the add-ons also the report deadlines of breaches and security incidents, data subject requests, the registration under NIS2 and follow-ups due. The command palette (`Ctrl + K`) finds assets, pages and actions; a scanned asset label or EAN appears there as the first match and opens the asset or the item with Enter.

## Inventory

- **Assets** with category, location, owners, lifecycle (planned, active, in maintenance, retired, disposed), protection requirement per security goal and relations to other assets.
- **Categories** as a tree with fields of their own per category, such as IMEI for mobile devices or, for **medical technology**, the risk class under the MDR, the due safety and metrological checks (German MPBetreibV) and the UDI-DI; fields holding personal values are marked and visible only to roles allowed to see them. The navigation lists the categories with their counts.
- **Locations** as a tree from site through building and floor down to the room, with a responsible person, a contact on site, access protection, hazards after the BSI's elementary threats and the internet connection. Connections between locations (WAN, VPN, own line, radio link) are seen from both ends. A location's protection requirement follows the maximum principle from its assets.
- **Inventory numbers** with numbering schemes per category, optionally required; batch creation of identical assets.
- **Print labels** with QR code, inventory number and name: on A4 sheets (3 × 8 labels of 70 × 37 mm), on tape (12, 18 or 24 mm) for label printers, or for the **DYMO LabelWriter** on labels 99012 (89 × 36 mm), 11354 (57 × 32 mm) and 11352 (54 × 25 mm). DYMO labels also carry the inventory number as a barcode (Code 128) for handheld scanners that read one-dimensional codes only; if a number is too long for the label, the QR code remains on its own. Printing goes through the browser's print dialog: the LabelWriter as printer, the label as paper, no margins and 100 %.
- **Scan assets** under **Assets › Scan**: a handheld scanner or the camera reads the label, the asset shows at once with category, location, status and inventory number, and the page is ready for the next scan. The camera uses the browser's barcode reader (Chromium-based browsers); the picture stays on the device. A bare inventory number read by the camera is searched for only once confirmed. Which handheld scanners suit and how to set their keyboard layout is described under [Handheld scanners](/en/guides/scanner/).
- **Asset list** with search, filters (category, device class, location including everything below it, health), bulk editing with "Undo" and CSV export with formula protection.
- **Asset detail** with profile, relations, history without personal values and health.
- **Documentation of assets and locations:** in the **Documentation** tab of an asset or location you write installation guides, runbooks and the like as Markdown with Mermaid diagrams, stored in versions like other documents; existing Markdown files can be imported, see [Documentation of assets and locations](/en/guides/dokumentation/).
- **Picture of an asset** on the overview, such as a photo of the device so it can be recognised on site: **Upload picture** takes any picture the browser can show, phone photos included. The browser shrinks it to at most 1600 pixels before the upload; stored are PNG or JPEG up to 2 MB. Browser and server remove where it was taken and other metadata. One picture per asset; whoever may edit the asset may change it, every role may view it. Removing can be undone, and deleting an asset deletes its picture too.
- **Dependencies:** every relation between assets on one page, filtered by kind and searchable.
- **GLPI import:** CSV exports with proposals for category, status and location, a check of every row and creation all or nothing.
- **Stocktaking** per location with everything below it: target frozen at the start, recording by search, label or inventory number, results found, missing, found elsewhere, not in target and unknown; unchangeable once closed, **Take over location** for assets found elsewhere, a protocol to print or save as a PDF. From Pro, a scan field records each scan at once. See [Stocktaking](/en/guides/inventur/).
- **Disposal with proof of erasure** after BSI CON.6: reason, data media with method (NIST SP 800-88, secure erase, cryptographic, destruction with a DIN 66399 security level, disposer), performed and checked by, proof as a document, whereabouts; unchangeable once closed, a proof can be handed in later but then no longer deleted, renamed or replaced by a new version; a protocol with signature fields. See [Disposal](/en/guides/aussonderung/).
- **Lifecycle rules:** warranty ending, a device older than its replacement age, not seen for long; as tasks and filters, without a rule changing an asset by itself.
- **Checks of medical devices:** when the safety check (STK) or metrological check (MTK) of a device in operation has passed, the task **Check overdue** appears (red), because without the check the device may no longer be used. When a check falls today or within the next 30 days, **Check due** appears; a device counts once, overdue first. Both are filters of the asset list, part of an e-mail subscription category and of what needs care in the inventory report. They read the date fields "Sicherheitstechnische Kontrolle fällig" and "Messtechnische Kontrolle fällig" of the medical technology category and of any own category with date fields under the keys `stk_due` and `mtk_due`; devices out of operation do not count.

## Automatic discovery

- **Agents** for Linux and Windows, see [Agents](/en/guides/agents/). To remove one, block the agent and delete it from the device with the command shown, certificate and keys included, see [Uninstalling an agent](/en/guides/agents/#uninstalling-an-agent).
- **Collector** for network scans with SNMPv3 and LLDP, see [Collector](/en/guides/collector/).
- **Software inventory** per device with normalised names and publishers; the overview filters by platform, source, publisher and device class.

### Directory

The **Active Directory** sync reads computer objects over LDAPS (port 636) from the chosen OUs, with an ordinary domain account without special rights. It matches them with assets; conflicts are resolved by hand. Domain controllers must be in an allowed network (`SA_DIRECTORY_ALLOWED_NETWORKS`). Set up under **Settings › Directory services**: save a draft, **Test connection**, activate. From Pro, [several domains or forests](/en/guides/active-directory/#several-domains-or-forests) are possible, and the same connections also read the [people of chosen groups](/en/pro/active-directory/), matches them with the responsible persons, sets the department from OU or office where needed and reports leavers and joiners.

## Asset health

A traffic light per asset (healthy, warning, critical, unknown) with its reasons, from the agent's last heartbeat with fixed thresholds (silence by device class, disk from 90 %, memory from 95 %) and from states an existing monitoring reports. Assets needing attention are on a page of their own and appear as tasks in the overview. The **Health** tab of an asset shows CPU, memory, disks, network throughput and graphics card.

**Security state of devices:** once a day the agent reports for each protective function whether it is on, off, partly on or unknown: disk encryption, antivirus (with the age of its signatures), firewall, automatic updates (with the day of the last updates), Secure Boot and screen lock. It reads states only, never keys, rules, findings or names of updates; what cannot be determined reliably with built-in means stays "unknown". The state shows in the **Health** tab and serves the implementation help in IT-Grundschutz as a suggestion. On macOS it follows with the macOS agent.

**Status tokens** (under **Settings**) let a monitoring report states only (`POST /api/v1/status-reports`), without reading or changing anything; they are shown once, kept as a hash, expire and can be revoked. See [API](/en/reference/api/).

## IT-Grundschutz

Grundschutz++ and Edition 2023 with a guided process, the Grundschutz check and evidence, see [IT-Grundschutz](/en/guides/grundschutz/).

## Records

- **Licences:** product, manufacturer, licence model (per device, per user, volume, concurrent use, subscription, site), seats, term and cost; assigned to assets or people, over-assignment shown; licence keys encrypted and visible only to administration and information security, each time with an audit entry.
- **Contracts:** supplier, type, term, notice period with automatic renewal, cost with billing interval; reminders before deadlines; covered assets, licences and locations.
- **Certificates:** import from PEM, DER or PKCS#7 (files with a private key are refused), chain and issuer from the signatures, deadlines 14, 30 and 60 days ahead, weak algorithms named, revocation lists checked daily.
- **Asset handovers and security keys:** handing out and taking back with condition and a protocol to print; a reminder before the last working day. From Pro, [joiners and leavers](/en/pro/ein-und-austritte/) lead through joining and leaving, with ready-made templates after BSI IT-Grundschutz and ISO/IEC 27001, and find what a person has to return; under [My devices](/en/pro/selbstbedienung/), employees see their devices, report damage and request what they need. For security keys and smartcards the form factor, functions, firmware, registered services and certificates in the PIV slots. PIN, PUK, seeds or recovery codes are never stored.
- **Consumables:** stock per location with a minimum; receipts, withdrawals and stocktakings that are never changed afterwards but booked back; assignment to the assets an item is used with, such as toner to printers, with stock and **Reorder** on the asset; an **EAN** per item with check digit, unique within the organisation, found through the search and the command palette and scanned in with a handheld scanner, see [Consumables](/en/guides/verbrauchsmaterial/). From Pro, [receive by scan](/en/guides/verbrauchsmaterial/#receive-by-scan) books packages scanned with a hand scanner or the camera in at once, with suggestions from [Open Icecat](/en/pro/icecat/) for unknown EANs, and the [reorder proposal](/en/guides/beschaffung/#reorder-proposal) creates orders per supplier for items below their minimum.
- **Procurement:** procurement requests under **Inventory › Procurement** with a running number and up to 100 positions, status from requested through approved or rejected to ordered and taken into the inventory; a printed form with logo and a box for the management's signature, the signed copy as evidence, a PDF and a mail file with the PDF attached; sending by e-mail through the organisation's mail server to allowed domains or, when switched on, to any address; links to assets and consumables; **orders** without a request or approval, numbered apart (`BE` next to `BA`), cancelled and resumed, filtered by kind; a **receipt** that books positions with consumables straight into the stock of a storage location, at most the quantity ordered and only once, with the number as voucher, from Pro also by [scanning the delivery](/en/guides/beschaffung/#scan-the-delivery); from Pro the receipt creates [devices as assets](/en/guides/beschaffung/#creating-devices-as-assets) in one step, with scanned serial numbers; see [Procurement](/en/guides/beschaffung/).
- **Cost centers** under **Master data › Cost centers** with code, name and a responsible person, archivable; assigned to assets, licences, contracts, requests and orders, with a filter in the lists and a column in the asset CSV export, see [Cost centers](/en/guides/kostenstellen/).
- **Racks:** to scale, front and rear, with devices and passive elements; mounting by dragging, arrow keys or form with a collision check; ports, cables with label, type, colour and length, also between racks; connected ports in the colour of their cable; ports from SNMP and cable suggestions from LLDP; print view and PNG export; a port list to print, with labels and descriptions changed right in it; **port labels** on tape (24, 18, 12 or 9 mm) for label printers such as the Brother PT-E560BT, as strips for the label field or one label per port, see [Racks and ports](/en/guides/racks/).
- **Suppliers and contacts** with CSV import, archiving, access requests and erasure of personal data.
- **Documents** with versions, per-file encryption, confidentiality levels, retention periods, optional virus scanning and links to assets, locations, suppliers, licences and contracts.

## Reports

- **Inventory:** assets by lifecycle, category and location, what needs care (no owner, orphaned, support ending, medical device checks overdue or due once there are any, shadow IT) and agent coverage; every figure leads to the filtered asset list.
- **Costs:** monthly and yearly costs of licences and contracts per currency, area and item, and by cost center with the year's procurement, as CSV and for printing.

## Notifications

Webhook and Gotify for chosen events, such as new or blocked agents, sign-ins with local accounts, changes to local accounts, sign-in changes, failed directory syncs or a broken audit chain. Messages carry no personal details. Targets over https with TLS 1.3 only; by default only addresses in the internet, a server in your own network needs `SA_NOTIFICATION_ALLOWED_NETWORKS`. Set up under **Settings › Notifications**.

**By e-mail**, every person who subscribes in their profile hears about the tasks of the bell, per category at once or once a day, in German or English. A mail names only tasks with their count and a link, as the person sees them in the app. Mails go out through the organisation's mail server, with TLS 1.3 only; set up under **Settings › E-mail**, see [Notifications by e-mail](/en/guides/benachrichtigungen/).

## Add-ons

Add-ons are licensed separately and appear as their own group in the navigation.

- **ISMS add-on** (with Pro or Enterprise): guides you through an information security management system according to BSI standards 200-1 to 200-3, see [ISMS add-on](/en/pro/isms/).
- **Data protection add-on** (with any edition): guides you step by step through the duties of the GDPR, from the record of processing activities to the activity report, see [Data protection add-on](/en/pro/datenschutz-add-on/).
- **NIS2 add-on** (with any edition): guides you through the duties under NIS2 and the BSIG, from checking whether you are covered through registration, measures and reports to the evidence, see [NIS2 add-on](/en/pro/nis2/).

Deadlines and follow-ups of the add-ons appear under **Tasks**, in the bell and, on request, by e-mail, only for people who may make changes in the add-on.

## Operations

- **Sign-in:** local accounts with password and mandatory TOTP, created by the administration with a setup link; optionally OIDC with any identity provider; six [roles](/en/reference/rollen/), from Pro [adjustable and extended by custom roles](/en/pro/rollen-anpassen/); see [Setup](/en/guides/einrichtung/#sign-in-local-accounts-and-oidc).
- **System health:** database, Valkey, job worker and virus scanner, the job queue, deadlines of the agent PKI, document storage and the last check of the audit chain.
- **Audit log** with hash chain, filters, names of people and export as JSON Lines, see [Security](/en/reference/sicherheit/#audit-log).
- **Backup and migration** (Community): in the dashboard under **Settings › Backup**, back up now, back up daily on a schedule and keep the newest, check, download and restore, with maintenance, a safety backup and automatic fallback; a warning in the bell for a missing backup and for every download; all of it by command as well. See [Backup and migration](/en/guides/sicherung/).
- **Web UI** in German and English, light and dark; **Wiki / docs** in the sidebar opens the guide for the current area, in English when the interface is English.
