SentryAssets has six fixed roles with default permissions; with [Pro](/en/pro/rollen-anpassen/) the administration adapts them and creates custom roles. For local accounts the administration sets the role under **Settings › Users** ([Local accounts](/en/guides/einrichtung/#local-accounts)); for OIDC accounts it comes from the identity provider's groups at every sign-in ([Sign-in with OIDC](/en/guides/einrichtung/#sign-in-with-oidc-optional)). Under **Settings › Roles and permissions**, administration, information security, data protection and auditors see the same table every request is checked against, with every adjustment of the organisation.

| Role | Key | Meant for |
|---|---|---|
| Administrator | `admin` | operations and setup: everything, including sign-in, users, agents, notifications, system health |
| Information security | `isb` | information security officers: inventory and IT-Grundschutz in full, audit log, licence keys |
| Editor | `editor` | the IT team day to day: inventory, locations, licences, contracts, documents, Grundschutz check |
| Data protection | `dpo` | data protection officers: read everything like auditors, plus personal fields, contacts and strictly confidential documents; change nothing in the core |
| Auditor (read only) | `auditor` | internal or external audit: read everything including the audit log, change nothing |
| Read only | `viewer` | information: read inventory and records |

If more than one group matches at sign-in, the first in this order applies: administrator, information security, editor, data protection, auditor, read only; a custom role counts with its base role, after the fixed role of the same base. Without a matching group the sign-in is refused.

## Confidentiality of documents

| Role | Sees documents up to |
|---|---|
| Administrator, information security, data protection | strictly confidential |
| Editor, auditor | confidential |
| Read only | internal |

Evidence above one's own level is only counted in the Grundschutz check, not shown.

## Rights per location

With [Pro](/en/pro/standortrechte/) people can be limited to writing at certain locations. Reading stays as the role allows.

## Adjusting and custom roles

With [Pro](/en/pro/rollen-anpassen/) the administration gives or takes permissions of single roles, changes the limit of confidentiality and creates up to 20 custom roles based on a fixed role. Permissions over users, sign-in, roles, licence and backups always stay with the administration alone. When the licence ends, the adjustments keep applying; only changing them is locked.
